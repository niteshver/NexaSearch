Based on these designs, we develop a single-stage multi-person pose estimation framework, termed ER-Pose. On MS COCO and CrowdPose, ER-Pose-n achieves AP improvements of 3.2/6.7 without pre-training and 7.4/4.9 with pre-training respectively compared with the baseline YOLO-Pose.
