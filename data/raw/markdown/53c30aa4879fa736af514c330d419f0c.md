# pandas.DatetimeIndex.year#

- property DatetimeIndex.year[source]#
- The year of the datetime. See also 
  - `DatetimeIndex.month`
  - The month as January=1, December=12.
  - `DatetimeIndex.day`
  - The day of the datetime.
 Examples >>> datetime_series = pd.Series( ... pd.date_range("2000-01-01", periods=3, freq="YE") ... ) >>> datetime_series 0 2000-12-31 1 2001-12-31 2 2002-12-31 dtype: datetime64[us] >>> datetime_series.dt.year 0 2000 1 2001 2 2002 dtype: int32
