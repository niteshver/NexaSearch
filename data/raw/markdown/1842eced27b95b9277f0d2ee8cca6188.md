    # Title:Resolvent estimates for non-selfadjoint operators with double characteristics

View PDF HTML (experimental)
            Abstract:We study resolvent estimates for non-selfadjoint semiclassical pseudodifferential operators with double characteristics. Assuming that the quadratic approximation along the double characteristics is elliptic, we obtain polynomial upper bounds on the resolvent in a suitable region inside the pseudospectrum.
