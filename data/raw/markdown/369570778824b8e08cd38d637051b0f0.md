    # Title:Thermal production of axino Dark Matter

View PDF HTML (experimental)
            Abstract:We reconsider thermal production of axinos in the early universe, adding: a) missed terms in the axino interaction; b) production via gluon decays kinematically allowed by thermal masses; c) a precise modeling of reheating. We find an axino abunance a few times larger than previous computations.
