    # Title:EMC effect in semi-inclusive deep-inelastic scattering process

View PDF HTML (experimental)
            Abstract:  By considering the xx-dependence of \pi^+π+, \pi^-π−, K^+K+, K^-K−, \LambdaΛ, \bar{\Lambda}Λ¯, pp, \bar{p}p¯ hadron productions in charged lepton semi-inclusive deep inelastic scattering off nuclear target (using Fe as an example) and deuteron D target, % at Q^2=5Q2=5 GeV^22, we find that (\bar{\Lambda}^A/\Lambda^A)/(\bar{\Lambda}^D/\Lambda^D)(Λ¯A/ΛA)/(Λ¯D/ΛD) and ({\bar{p}}^A/{p}^A)/({\bar{p}}^A/p^A)(p¯A/pA)/(p¯A/pA) are ideal to figure out the nuclear sea content, which is predicted to be different by different models accounting for the nuclear EMC effect.
