    # Title:Singularities of Affine Schubert Varieties

View PDF HTML (experimental)
            Abstract:  This paper studies the singularities of affine Schubert varieties in the affine Grassmannian (of type A(1)ℓ). For two classes of affine Schubert varieties, we determine the singular loci; and for one class, we also determine explicitly the tangent spaces at singular points. For a general affine Schubert variety, we give partial results on the singular locus.
