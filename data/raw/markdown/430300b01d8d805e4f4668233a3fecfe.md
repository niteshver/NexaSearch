    # Title:Local theta correspondence and the lifting of Duke, Imamoglu and Ikeda

View PDF HTML (experimental)
            Abstract:  We use results on the local theta correspondence to prove that for large degrees the Duke-Imamoglu-Ikeda lifting of an elliptic modular form is not a linear combination of theta series.
