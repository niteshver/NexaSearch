# Title:Smooth approximation of Lipschitz projections

View PDF HTML (experimental)
            Abstract:  We show that any Lipschitz projection-valued function p on a connected closed Riemannian manifold can be approximated uniformly by smooth projection-valued functions q with Lipschitz constant close to that of p. This answers a question of Rieffel.
