#  Title:Physics-Informed Neural Operator for Warm-Starting Background-Decomposed and Preconditioned PSFD: Enabling Scalable 3-D EUV Mask Simulation
View a PDF of the paper titled Physics-Informed Neural Operator for Warm-Starting Background-Decomposed and Preconditioned PSFD: Enabling Scalable 3-D EUV Mask Simulation, by Doyun Kim and 1 other authors
View PDF HTML (experimental)
> Abstract:We present a physics-informed neural operator (PINO) trained with pseudo-spectral frequency-domain (PSFD) equations for electromagnetic (EM) scattering problems in EUV lithography. The Fourier neural operator is factorized into a two-dimensional lateral () branch and a one-dimensional axial () branch and is trained self-consistently with background for the scattered intensity of held-out mask patterns relative to the reference PSFD solution. Combined with spectral damping, the PINO warm-start initialization accelerates the background-decomposed PSFD solver on finer discretizations.
