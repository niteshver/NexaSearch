    # Title:Cohomology of the toroidal compactification of A_3

View PDF HTML (experimental)
            Abstract:  We prove that the cohomology groups with rational coefficients of the Voronoi compactification of the moduli space of abelian threefolds coincide with the Chow groups of that space, as determined by Van der Geer.
