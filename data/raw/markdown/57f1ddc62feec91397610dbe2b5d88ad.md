#   ⟨59⟩ Modern AI-Generated Fake Review Detection 
Binary corpus for detecting **present-day LLM-generated fake product reviews**.
  * **OR (genuine)** : real human Amazon-style reviews (the authentic half of the Salminen et al. Kaggle "Fake Reviews" dataset).
  * **CG (fake)** : fresh fake reviews generated by **GLM-5.2**. Each fake is _grounded on a paired real review_ (same category and star rating, length matched) and prompted to be a different, believable review for the same product — so category/rating/length marginals match the genuine half and the task is not solvable by superficial cues.


##   ⟨52⟩ Why a new dataset 
A detector trained on the original Kaggle "computer-generated" (GPT-2-era) half flags only ~4% of modern-LLM reviews — those labels capture obsolete generator-specific artifacts. This corpus replaces the machine half with current LLM output to study detection of _today's_ AI fakes.
##   ⟨53⟩ Preprocessing 
Both halves are normalised to a single ASCII punctuation convention (smart quotes/em-dashes -> ASCII) so Unicode is not a spurious tell. Exact and cross-class duplicates removed; classes balanced 1:1.
##   ⟨54⟩ Generation protocol and provenance 
Each generated review is grounded on the first 400 characters of a genuine review. Source order uses seed 1998, and the review angle uses an RNG seeded by the source-row index. Generation allows up to five attempts with temperatures 0.85, 0.91, 0.97, 1.03, and 1.09; `top_p=0.95`. Accepted outputs contain at least three words, stay within the recorded proportional length ceiling, and have source/generated word-set Jaccard similarity below 0.6.
No API decoding seed was supplied. Provider model aliases may be mutable, and some provider-side revisions were not recorded. The committed raw CSVs are the canonical artifacts. Exact system/user prompts, commands, artifact SHA-256 values, generation history, and known unknowns are recorded in `DATA_PROVENANCE.md`⟨60⟩ and `dataset/provenance.json`⟨61⟩.

##   ⟨56⟩ Splits 
`train` / `validation` / `test` reproduce the frozen protocol split (seed 1998: 80/20, then the 20% is split 30/70 into validation/test).
##   ⟨57⟩ Fields 
`text_` (review text), `label` (OR genuine / CG modern-AI-fake), `category`, `rating`.
##   ⟨58⟩ Known limitations 
  * "CG" denotes machine-generated, not necessarily deceptive in intent.
  * Generation used a small set of "review angles"; one (shipping/first-impressions) mildly over-represents delivery/packaging vocabulary in the fake half. This is a residual bias, documented for transparency.
  * In-distribution detection is easy (bag-of-words ~99%); the intended use is cross-generator / robustness and short-review evaluation, where a contextual model measurably outperforms surface features.
