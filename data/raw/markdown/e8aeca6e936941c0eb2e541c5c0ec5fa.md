A dataset for classification of sugarcane damage. The dataset contains 153 images across 6 classes: cracked, crushed, no_buds, no_damage, single_damaged_buds, two_buds.  
Images per class:
  * cracked: 24
  * crushed: 24
  * no_buds: 21
  * no_damage: 36
  * single_damaged_buds: 24
  * two_buds: 24
