# pandas.tseries.offsets.BYearBegin.month#

- BYearBegin.month#
- Return the month of the year on which this offset applies. Returns an integer representing the month (1-12) that this offset targets. For year-based offsets, this determines which month is used for calculations. See also 
  - `tseries.offsets.YearEnd`
  - Offset to end of year.
  - `tseries.offsets.YearBegin`
  - Offset to start of year.
  - `tseries.offsets.BYearEnd`
  - Offset to last business day of year.
  - `tseries.offsets.BYearBegin`
  - Offset to first business day of year.
 Examples >>> pd.offsets.BYearBegin().month 1 >>> pd.offsets.BYearBegin(month=6).month 6
