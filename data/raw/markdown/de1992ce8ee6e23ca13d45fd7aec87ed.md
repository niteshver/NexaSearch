# Title:Effect of curvature squared corrections in AdS on the viscosity of the dual gauge theory

View PDF HTML (experimental)
            Abstract:  We use the real-time finite-temperature AdS/CFT correspondence to compute the effect of general R^2 corrections to the gravitational action in AdS space on the shear viscosity of the dual gauge theory. One such contribution, that appears for finite N, is determined by the central charges of the CFT. It seems that the conjectured lower bound of 1/(4\pi) on the viscosity-to-entropy ratio can be violated.
