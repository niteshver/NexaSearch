#   ⟨150⟩ CitrusUAT Disease Classification 
A dataset for disease classification of orange leaves. The dataset contains 953 images across 12 classes: Citrus_leafminer, Fe, Greasy_spot, HLB, Healthy, Mg, Mn, N, Red_scale, Red_scale_sequelae, Texas_mite, Zn.  
Images per class:
  * Citrus_leafminer: 100
  * Fe: 100
  * Greasy_spot: 100
  * HLB: 43
  * Healthy: 100
  * Mg: 100
  * Mn: 30
  * N: 50
  * Red_scale: 30
  * Red_scale_sequelae: 100
  * Texas_mite: 100
  * Zn: 100


This dataset is indexed on <https://project-agml.github.io/> as part of the AgML python library.
