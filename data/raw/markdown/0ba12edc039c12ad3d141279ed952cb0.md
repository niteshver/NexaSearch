# Our Members
We are so thankful for our members – arXiv would not be possible without their support. arXiv members are universities, research labs, libraries, and consortia that contribute funding to arXiv to help advance the cause of open science.
Other funding for arXiv comes from foundations, affiliates, and sponsors.
Interested in supporting arXiv and expanding the possibilities of open science? Learn more about arXiv's funding and contact us at membership@arXiv.org.
