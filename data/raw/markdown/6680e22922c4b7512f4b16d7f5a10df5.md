##   ⟨49⟩ 1. Winner decision (v3 vs v4 at 5B) 
v4 must beat v3 in the 5B head-to-head to earn 30B escalation:
  * v4 loss ≤ v3 loss + 0.05 (strict regression check)
  * v4 ≥ v3 - 0.02 in ≥ 7/12 short-L cells (4K/16K × 6 tasks)
  * v4 4K single_1 ≥ 0.30 (collapse floor)
  * Long-L bonus (32K/64K/128K): v4 우위 logged as evidence, not required


PASS → winner=v4 → 30B v4 escalation. FAIL → winner=v3 → 30B v3 escalation.
##   ⟨50⟩ 2. What's new in v4 
Two architectural improvements on top of v3's leak-fixed foundation:
  1. **Learnable attention-pool descriptor** (`desc_attn_v`): replaces the fixed [mean/max/softmax-attn] triplet with a learned attention pool so the descriptor can highlight key-relevant features per chunk.
  2. **Entropy bias on routing logits** (`ent_bias_scale`): adds a bias proportional to batch-level routing entropy, letting the router sharpen or smooth its decisions dynamically.


##   ⟨51⟩ 3. v4 source swap 
v4 is applied at training time by copying `dsc/v4/dsc.py` over `off/GatedDeltaNet-2/lit_gpt/dsc.py` (with a trap to restore v3 on exit). See `pretrain_dsc_370m_5b_v4.sh` and `pretrain_dsc_370m_30b_v4.sh`.
