    # Title:Actor of an alternative algebra

View PDF HTML (experimental)
            Abstract:  We define a category \galt$\galt$ of g-alternative algebras over a field FF and present the category of alternative algebras \alt$\alt$ as a full subcategory of \galt$\galt$; in the case \ch F\neq 2$\ch F\neq 2$, we have \alt=\galt$\alt=\galt$. For any g-alternative algebra AA we give a construction of a universal strict general actor \cB(A)$\cB(A)$ of AA. We define the subset \asoci(A)$\asoci(A)$ of AA, and show that it is a \cB(A)$\cB(A)$-substructure of AA. We prove that if \asoci(A)=0$\asoci(A)=0$, then there exists an actor of AA in \galt$\galt$ and \act(A)=\cB(A)$\act(A)=\cB(A)$. In particular, we obtain that if AA is anticommutative and \ann(A)=0$\ann(A)=0$, then there exists an actor of AA in \galt$\galt$; from this, under the same conditions, we deduce the existence of an actor in \alt$\alt$.
