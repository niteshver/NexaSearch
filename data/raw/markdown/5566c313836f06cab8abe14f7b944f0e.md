    # Title:General isotropic flags are general (for Grassmannian Schubert calculus)

View PDF HTML (experimental)
            Abstract:  We show that general isotropic flags for odd-orthogonal and symplectic groups are general for Schubert calculus on the classical Grassmannian in that Schubert cells defined by such flags meet transversally. This strengthens a result of Belkale and Kumar in arXiv:0708.0398.
