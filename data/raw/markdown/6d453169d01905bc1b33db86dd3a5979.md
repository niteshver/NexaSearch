# Title:Transport Properties of QCD at Large Nc and the Gauge/String Duality

View PDF HTML (experimental)
            Abstract:  At temperatures below the deconfinement phase transition, QCD at large Nc is expected to be a very viscous hadronic fluid because both the shear and bulk viscosity to entropy density ratio, $\eta/s,\zeta/s \sim \order{N_c^2}$. In this note I show that the expected N2c dependence of η/s in the confined phase of QCD at large Nc may not captured by the gauge/string duality even after the inclusion of stringy or quantum corrections to the usual Einstein-like supergravity actions. I argue that a non-perturbative description of finite temperature QCD at large Nc (and zero baryonic chemical potential) obtained using gravity duals provide reliable results only near the deconfinement phase transition where the relevant transport coefficients, such as η/s, are $\sim \order{1}$.
