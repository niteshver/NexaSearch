# pandas.PeriodIndex.second#

- property PeriodIndex.second[source]#
- The second of the period. Returns the second component for each period in the index. See also 
  - `PeriodIndex.hour`
  - The hour of the period.
  - `PeriodIndex.minute`
  - The minute of the period.
  - `PeriodIndex.to_timestamp`
  - Cast to DatetimeArray/Index.
 Examples >>> idx = pd.PeriodIndex(["2023-01-01 10:00:30", ... "2023-01-01 10:00:31"], freq='s') >>> idx.second Index([30, 31], dtype='int64')
