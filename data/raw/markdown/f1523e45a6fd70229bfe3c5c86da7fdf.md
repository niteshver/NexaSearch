    # Title:Quantum criticality and the phase diagram of the cuprates

View PDF HTML (experimental)
            Abstract:  I discuss a proposed phase diagram of the cuprate superconductors as a function of temperature, carrier concentration, and a strong magnetic field perpendicular to the layers. I show how the phase diagram gives a unified interpretation of a number of recent experiments.
