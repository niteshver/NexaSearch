#   ⟨115⟩ European Automotive & Vehicle Heritage Dataset — 49 Authentic Details 
##   ⟨94⟩ 📋 Description 
Curated collection of **49 high-resolution photographs** documenting authentic European automotive and vehicle heritage — from iconic vintage cars and classic motorcycles to military armored vehicles, construction machinery, horse-drawn carts, toy models, and public transport.
Each image includes comprehensive **CSV metadata** with 17 classification fields optimized for machine learning pipelines, generative AI training, and computer vision benchmarks.
###   ⟨95⟩ What makes this dataset unique 
  * **Authenticity** : All photographs shot on-location across Europe — no stock imagery, no synthetic generation
  * **Real locations** : Shot primarily in Italy (46), plus Germany (1), Southeast Asia (1), Africa (1)
  * **Genuine condition** : 36 of 49 vehicles in pristine/restored condition, 6 in fair condition, 2 weathered — capturing authentic patina and wear
  * **Vehicle diversity** : Cars (28), motorcycles (5), military vehicles (6), construction equipment (4), horse-drawn carriages (2), toy models (4), rail/tram (1)
  * **Rich metadata** : ~41 descriptive keywords per image + 17 structured classification fields
  * **No missing data** : All 17 metadata fields are fully populated for every image
