    # Title:Metric groups attached to biextensions

View PDF HTML (experimental)
            Abstract:Let G be a connnected, unipotent, perfect group scheme over an algebraically closed field of characteristic p > 0. V. Drinfeld has defined a certain metric group associated to biextensions of G \times G by the discrete group Q_p/Z_p. We prove a certain conjecture of Drinfeld regarding the class of this metric group in the Witt group.
