    # Title:A Dispersion Relation for the Density of States With Application to the Casimir Effect

View PDF HTML (experimental)
            Abstract:The trace of a function of a Schrodinger operator minus the same for the Laplacian can be expressed in terms of the determinant of its scattering matrix. The naive formula for this determinant is divergent. Using a dispersion relation, we find another expression for it which is convergent, but needs one piece of information beyond the scattering matrix. Except for this `anomaly', we can express the Casimir energy of a compact body in terms of its optical scattering matrix, without assuming any rotational symmetry for its shape.
