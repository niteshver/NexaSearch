    # Title:Strong decays of the newly observed D(2550)D(2550), D(2600)D(2600), D(2750)D(2750), and D(2760)D(2760)

View PDF HTML (experimental)
            Abstract:The strong decay properties of the newly observed D(2550)D(2550), D(2600)D(2600), D(2750)D(2750) and D(2760)D(2760) are studied in a constituent quark model. It is predicted that the D(2760)D(2760) and D(2750)D(2750) seem to be two overlapping resonances. The D(2760)D(2760) could be identified as the 1^3D_313D3 with J^P=3^-JP=3−, while the D(2750)D(2750) is most likely to be the high-mass mixed state |1{D_2}'>_H (J^P=2^-) via the 1^1D_2-1^3D_2 mixing. The D(2600) favors the low-mass mixed state |(SD)_1>_L (J^P=1^-) via the 1^3D_1-2^3S_1 mixing. The D(2550) as the 2^1S_0 assignment bears controversies for its too broad width given in experiments.
