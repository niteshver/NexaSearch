    # Title:On WZ-pairs which prove Ramanujan series

View PDF HTML (experimental)
            Abstract:The known WZ-proofs for Ramanujan-type series related to 1/\pi gave us the insight to develop a new proof strategy based on the WZ-method. Using this approach we are able to find more generalizations and discover first WZ-proofs for certain series of this type.
