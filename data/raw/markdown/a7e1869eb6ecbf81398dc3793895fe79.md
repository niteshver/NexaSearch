    # Title:Multiplicative updates For Non-Negative Kernel SVM

View PDF HTML (experimental)
            Abstract:  We present multiplicative updates for solving hard and soft margin support vector machines (SVM) with non-negative kernels. They follow as a natural extension of the updates for non-negative matrix factorization. No additional param- eter setting, such as choosing learning, rate is required. Ex- periments demonstrate rapid convergence to good classifiers. We analyze the rates of asymptotic convergence of the up- dates and establish tight bounds. We test the performance on several datasets using various non-negative kernels and report equivalent generalization errors to that of a standard SVM.
