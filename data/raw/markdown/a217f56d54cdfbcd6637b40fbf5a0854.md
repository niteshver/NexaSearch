# Title:The smooth structure set of Sp×Sq

View PDF HTML (experimental)
            Abstract:  We calculate the smooth structure set of Sp×Sq, S(p,q), for p,q≥2 and p+q≥5. As a consequence we show that in general S(4j−1,4k) cannot admit a group structure such that the smooth surgery exact sequence is a long exact sequence of groups. We also show that the image of forgetful map F:S(4j,4k)−−>STop(4j,4k) is not in general a subgroup of the topological structure set.
