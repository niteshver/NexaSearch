    # Title:Elementary Proof for Asymptotics of Large Haar-Distributed Unitary Matrices

View PDF HTML (experimental)
            Abstract:  We provide an elementary proof for a theorem due to Petz and Réffy which states that for a random n\times n unitary matrix with distribution given by the Haar measure on the unitary group U(n), the upper left (or any other) k\times k submatrix converges in distribution, after multiplying by a normalization factor \sqrt{n} and as n\to\infty, to a matrix of independent complex Gaussian random variables with mean 0 and variance 1.
