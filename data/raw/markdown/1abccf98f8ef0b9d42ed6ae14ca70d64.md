#   ⟨95⟩ Synthetic Indian Driving License Image Dataset 
##   ⟨80⟩ Overview 
This dataset contains **30 synthetic images** of Indian driving licenses generated using the Z-Image-Turbo⟨96⟩ diffusion model. The images cover a wide range of Indian states/UTs, document formats, lighting conditions, angles, and wear states — designed for training and evaluating document analysis, OCR, and classification models.

##   ⟨90⟩ Intended Use 
This dataset is intended for:
  * Document detection and classification
  * OCR research on Indian identity documents
  * Document quality assessment
  * Synthetic data augmentation for document analysis pipelines


##   ⟨91⟩ Limitations 
  * Images are **synthetic** and may not perfectly represent real Indian driving licenses
  * Text content in images is AI-generated and may not be readable/accurate
  * The dataset is small (30 images) and should be combined with real data for production use
  * No personal data of real individuals is contained in this dataset
