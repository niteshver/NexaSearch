**OpenFWI: Large-scale Multi-structural Benchmark Datasets for Full Waveform Inversion**
OpenFWI is the first collection of large-scale, multi-structural benchmark datasets for machine learning driven seismic Full Wave Inversion (FWI). It includes eleven 2D datasets and one 3D dataset, in four subsurface structure families. Here, We provide three of them (Vel, Fault, and Style). For both 2D and 3D Kimberlina datasets, please refer to OpenFWI⟨38⟩ for more details.
