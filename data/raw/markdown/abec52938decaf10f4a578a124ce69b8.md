# Title:EPR, Bell, and Quantum Locality

View PDF HTML (experimental)
            Abstract:Maudlin has claimed, Am. J. Phys. 78 (2010) p. 121, that no local theory can reproduce the predictions of standard quantum mechanics that violate Bell's inequality in the case of Bohm's version (two spin-half particles in a singlet state) of the Einstein-Podolsky-Rosen problem. It is argued that, on the contrary, standard quantum mechanics itself is a counterexample to Maudlin's claim, as it is local in the appropriate sense (measurements here do not influence what is going on over there) when formulated using consistent principles in place of the inconsistent appeals to "measurement" found in current textbooks. This sheds some light on the claim of Blaylock (ibid. p. 111) that counterfactual definiteness is an essential ingredient in derivations of Bell's inequality.
