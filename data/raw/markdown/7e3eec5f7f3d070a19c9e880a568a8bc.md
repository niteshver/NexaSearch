    # Title:Companions of the unknot and width additivity

View PDF HTML (experimental)
            Abstract:  It has been conjectured that for knots K and K′ in S3, $w(K#K')= w(K)+w(K')-2$. Scharlemann and Thompson have proposed potential counterexamples to this conjecture. For every n, they proposed a family of knots Kni for which they conjectured that $w(B^n#K^n_i)=w(K^n_i)$ where Bn is a bridge number n knot. We show that for n>2 none of the knots in Kni produces such counterexamples.
