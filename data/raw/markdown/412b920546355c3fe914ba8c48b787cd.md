    # Title:Bounds on the Hilbert-Kunz Multiplicity

View PDF HTML (experimental)
            Abstract:In this paper we give new lower bounds on the Hilbert-Kunz multiplicity of unmixed non-regular local rings, bounding them uniformly away from one. Our results improve previous work of Aberbach and Enescu.
