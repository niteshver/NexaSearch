    # Title:Poincaré inequality for Markov random fields via disagreement percolation

View PDF HTML (experimental)
            Abstract:We consider Markov random fields of discrete spins on the lattice \Zd. We use a technique of coupling of conditional distributions. If under the coupling the disagreement cluster is "sufficiently" subcritical, then we prove the Poincaré inequality. In the whole subcritical regime, we have a weak Poincaré inequality and corresponding polynomial upper bound for the relaxation of the associated Glauber dynamics.
