# Title:Observation of self-localized and propagating spin wave modes in obliquely magnetized current-driven magnetic nanocontacts

View PDF HTML (experimental)
            Abstract:  It is experimentally demonstrated that in an obliquely magnetized current-driven magnetic nano-contact two spin wave modes, with different frequencies and opposite signs of the nonlinear frequency shift, can be excited. The lower frequency mode is excited only when the magnetic field makes a sufficiently small angle θe < 50 deg with the film plane. It has a negative (red) nonlinear frequency shift, and is identified as a self-localized nonlinear spin wave ``bullet''. In contrast, the higher frequency mode can be excited at almost any values of θe. It has a positive (blue) nonlinear frequency shift, and it is identified as the exchange-dominated propagating spin wave mode. Theoretical calculations of the mode frequencies and threshold currents, support this interpretation.
