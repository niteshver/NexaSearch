# pandas.Period.day#

- Period.day#
- Get day of the month that a Period falls on. 
  - Returns:
    - int
 See also 
  - `Period.dayofweek`
  - Get the day of the week.
  - `Period.dayofyear`
  - Get the day of the year.
 Examples >>> p = pd.Period("2018-03-11", freq='h') >>> p.day 11
