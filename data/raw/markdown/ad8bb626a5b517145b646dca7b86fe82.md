    # Title:Jacobi quasi-Nijenhuis Algebroids

View PDF HTML (experimental)
            Abstract:In this paper, for a Jacobi algebroid A, by introducing the notion of Jacobi quasi-Nijenhuis algebroids, which is a generalization of Poisson quasi-Nijenhuis manifolds introduced by Stiénon and Xu, we study generalized complex structures on the Courant-Jacobi algebroid A\oplus A^*, which unifies generalized complex (contact) structures on an even(odd)-dimensional manifold.
