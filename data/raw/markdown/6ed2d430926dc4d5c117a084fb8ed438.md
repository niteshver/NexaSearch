    # Title:Concentration of measures via size biased couplings

View PDF HTML (experimental)
            Abstract:Let Y be a nonnegative random variable with mean \mu and finite positive variance \sigma^2, and let Y^s, defined on the same space as Y, have the Y size biased distribution, that is, the distribution characterized by E[Yf(Y)]=\mu E f(Y^s) for all functions f for which these expectations exist. Under a variety of conditions on the coupling of Y and Y^s, including combinations of boundedness and monotonicity, concentration of measure inequalities hold. Examples include the number of relatively ordered subsequences of a random permutation, sliding window statistics including the number of m-runs in a sequence of coin tosses, the number of local maximum of a random function on a lattice, the number of urns containing exactly one ball in an urn allocation model, the volume covered by the union of n balls placed uniformly over a volume n subset of d dimensional Euclidean space, the number of bulbs switched on at the terminal time in the so called lightbulb process, and the infinitely divisible and compound Poisson distributions that satisfy a bounded moment generating function condition.
