    # Title:P-spectrum and collapsing of connected sums, calculus of the limit

View PDF HTML (experimental)
            Abstract:  The goal of the paper is to calculate the limit sectrum of the Hodge-Laplace operator under the perturbation of collapse of one part of a connected sum. This gives some new results concerning the 'conformal spectrum' on differential forms.
