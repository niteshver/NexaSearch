    # Title:Experimental Test of Bell inequalities with Six-Qubit Graph States

View PDF HTML (experimental)
            Abstract:We report on the experimental realization of two different Bell inequality tests based on six-qubit linear-type and Y-shape graph states. For each of these states, the Bell inequalities tested are optimal in the sense that they provide the maximum violation among all Bell inequalities with stabilizing observables and possess the maximum resistance to noise.
