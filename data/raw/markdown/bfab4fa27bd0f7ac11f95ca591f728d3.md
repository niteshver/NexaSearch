    # Title:Probabilistic characterisation of Besov-Lipschitz spaces on metric measure spaces

View PDF HTML (experimental)
            Abstract:  We give a probabilistic characterisation of the Besov-Lipschitz spaces Lip(α,p,q)(X) on domains which support a Markovian kernel with appropriate exponential bounds. This extends former results of \cite{Jon,KPP1,KPP2,GHL} which were valid for α=dw2,p=2, q=∞, where dw is the walk dimension of the space X.
