# pandas.PeriodIndex.quarter#

- property PeriodIndex.quarter[source]#
- The quarter of the date. See also 
  - `PeriodIndex.qyear`
  - Fiscal year the Period lies in according to its starting-quarter.
 Examples >>> idx = pd.PeriodIndex(["2023-01", "2023-02", "2023-03"], freq="M") >>> idx.quarter Index([1, 1, 1], dtype='int64')
