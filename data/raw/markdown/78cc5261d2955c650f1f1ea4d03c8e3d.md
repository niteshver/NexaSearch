    # Title:Light Squarks and Gluinos at TeV-scale e+e− Colliders

View PDF HTML (experimental)
            Abstract:In the general MSSM, first and second generation squarks and gluinos may be sufficiently light to be produced and studied at e+e− colliders operating in the 0.5-1 TeV energy range. After a reminder that the MSSM is {\it not} the same as mSUGRA, we provide a brief overview of these possibilities within this more general framework
