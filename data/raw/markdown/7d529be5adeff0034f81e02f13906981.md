    # Title:Bilipschitz equivalence is not equivalent to quasi-isometric equivalence for finitely generated groups

View PDF HTML (experimental)
            Abstract:  We show that certain lamplighter groups that are quasi-isometric to each other are not bilipschitz equivalent. This gives a positive answer to a question in Topics in Geometric Group Theory by Pierre de la Harpe (page 107).
