# Title:Bone Cancer in Dinosaurs

View PDF
            Abstract:  We test the prevalence of bone cancer in dinosaurs as determined from radiological examination of fossils against the rate extrapolated from modern vertebrates, and find that there is no evidence of a different rate, and therefore no supporting evidence for any role for extraterrestrial radiation sources.
