    # Title:Garside groups and Yang-Baxter equation

View PDF HTML (experimental)
            Abstract:We establish a one-to-one correspondence between a class of Garside groups admitting a certain presentation and the structure groups of non-degenerate, involutive and braided set-theoretical solutions of the quantum Yang-Baxter equation. We also characterize indecomposable solutions in terms of Δ-pure Garside groups.
