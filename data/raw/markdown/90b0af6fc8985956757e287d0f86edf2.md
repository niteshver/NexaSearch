# Title:Hidden sector effects on double higgs production near threshold

View PDF HTML (experimental)
            Abstract:In this letter we study a novel effect of a hidden sector coupling to the standard model Higgs boson on the process of double Higgs production via gluon fusion. After summing the ladder contributions of the hidden sector to the effective ggHH coupling, we find the amplitude for gluon-gluon scattering via a Higgs loop. We relate this amplitude to the double Higgs production cross section via the optical theorem. In our examples we find enhancement factors as large as 1000 near the threshold region. The detection of such an effect could in principle lead to important information about the hidden sector.
