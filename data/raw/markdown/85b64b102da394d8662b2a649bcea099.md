    # Title:Reducible connections and non-local symmetries of the self-dual Yang-Mills equations

View PDF HTML (experimental)
            Abstract:  We construct the most general reducible connection that satisfies the self-dual Yang-Mills equations on a simply connected, open subset of flat \mathbb{R}^4. We show how all such connections lie in the orbit of the flat connection on \mathbb{R}^4 under the action of non-local symmetries of the self-dual Yang-Mills equations. Such connections fit naturally inside a larger class of solutions to the self-dual Yang-Mills equations that are analogous to harmonic maps of finite type.
