    # Title:Discovery of the Color Degree of Freedom in Particle Physics: a Personal Perspective

            Abstract:  I review the main features of the color charge degree of freedom in particle physics, sketch the paradox in the early quark model that led to color, give a personal perspective on the discovery of color and describe the introduction of the gauge theory of color.
