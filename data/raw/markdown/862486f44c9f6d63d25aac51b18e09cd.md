# About The Quantitative Finance archive
The Quantitative Finance archive (q-fin) formed on 1 December 2008 and you can read about the announcement of the archive here. Quantitative Finance expanded with new categories in February 2014. For more detailed background and explanation of the objectives, see the original q-fin announcement.
You can browse articles and view categories at the main quantitative finance archive page.
