#   ⟨48⟩ Clean_One_Speaker_SADA_Split 
Train/validation/test version of Sebssihakim/Clean_One_Speaker_SADA⟨49⟩, a cleaned single-speaker subset of the SADA corpus (SDAIA / Saudi Broadcasting Authority).
##   ⟨45⟩ Splits 
80/10/10, stratified by `speaker_dialect`, seed 42: train ~30.8k, validation ~3.9k, test ~3.9k rows.
  * The `Maghrebi` dialect (7 rows) was removed — too few samples to stratify.
  * Splits are segment-level: the same source show/speaker may appear in more than one split.
  * Rare dialects (Yemeni, Shamali, Janubi) have very few validation/test samples; per-dialect metrics on them are not statistically meaningful.


##   ⟨46⟩ Columns 
`audio`, `text`, `cleaned_text`, `speaker_age`, `speaker_gender`, `speaker_dialect`
