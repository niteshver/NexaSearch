# Title:Stable Poisson Graphs in One Dimension

View PDF HTML (experimental)
            Abstract:Let each point of a homogeneous Poisson process on \RR independently be equipped with a random number of stubs (half-edges) according to a given probability distribution \mu on the positive integers. We consider two natural schemes for perfectly matching the stubs to obtain a simple graph with degree distribution \mu, both derived from Gale-Shapley stable marriage. We prove results on the existence of an infinite component and on the length of the edges, with focus on the case \mu(\{2\})=1.
