# pandas.Period.weekofyear#

- Period.weekofyear#
- Get the week of the year on the given Period. 
  - Returns:
    - int
 See also 
  - `Period.dayofweek`
  - Get the day component of the Period.
  - `Period.weekday`
  - Get the day component of the Period.
 Examples >>> p = pd.Period("2018-03-11", "h") >>> p.weekofyear 10 >>> p = pd.Period("2018-02-01", "D") >>> p.weekofyear 5 >>> p = pd.Period("2018-01-06", "D") >>> p.weekofyear 1
