# Title:(GL(n+1,R),GL(n,R)) is a Gelfand pair

View PDF HTML (experimental)
            Abstract:  Consider the standard embedding of GL(n,R) to GL(n+1,R) and the two-sided action of GL(n,R) \times GL(n,R) on GL(n+1,R).

In this paper we show that any GL(n,R) \times GL(n,R)-invariant distribution on GL(n+1,R) is invariant with respect to transposition.

We show that this implies that the pair (GL(n+1,R),GL(n,R)) is a Gelfand pair. Namely, for any irreducible admissible continuous representation (\pi,E) of GL(n+1,R) on a Hilbert space E, dimHom_{GL(n,R)}(E^{\infty},C) \leq 1. We also prove an analogous result for the pair (GL(n+1,C),GL(n,C)).
