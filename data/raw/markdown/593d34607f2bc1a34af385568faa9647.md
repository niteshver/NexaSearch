    # Title:Thermodynamical quantities for overlap fermions with chemical potential

View PDF HTML (experimental)
            Abstract:  Recently a formulation of overlap fermions at finite density based on an analytic continuation of the sign function was proposed. We study this proposal by analyzing the energy and number densities for free fermions as a function of the chemical potential and the temperature. Our results show that overlap fermions with chemical potential give rise to the correct continuum behavior.
