    # Title:Compact Kähler manifolds with elliptic homotopy type

View PDF HTML (experimental)
            Abstract:  Simply connected compact Kähler manifolds of dimension up to three with elliptic homotopy type are characterized in terms of their Hodge diamonds. This is applied to classify the simply connected Kähler surfaces and Fano threefolds with elliptic homotopy type.
