#   ⟨63⟩ QA Base Data 
Normalized and paraphrased splits of 21 standard NLP benchmarks in **English** , **German** , **French** , **Spanish** , and **Italian** , intended for base model pretraining.
##   ⟨58⟩ Generation 
  * English: paraphrased with `Qwen3.5-27B-FP8` (April 2026)
  * German: translated and refined with `Qwen3.5-27B-FP8` (April 2026)
  * French: translated and refined with `Qwen3.5-27B-FP8` (May 2026)
  * Spanish: translated and refined with `Qwen3.5-27B-FP8` (May 2026)
  * Italian: translated and refined with `Qwen3.5-27B-FP8` (May 2026)


##   ⟨59⟩ Format 
All files are JSONL with a single field:

```
{"text": "..."}

```

Text is formatted as a self-contained passage combining question, options where applicable, and answer.
##   ⟨60⟩ Structure 

```
en/  English paraphrased benchmarks
de/  German translated and refined benchmarks
fr/  French translated and refined benchmarks
es/  Spanish translated and refined benchmarks
it/  Italian translated and refined benchmarks

```

Each language folder mirrors the same directory structure with 21 benchmark collections and 48 JSONL files.
