    # Title:Observation of the Y(4140) structure in the J/ψ\,ϕ Mass Spectrum in B^\pm\to J/ψ\,ϕK cays

View PDF HTML (experimental)
            Abstract:The observation of the Y(4140) structure in B^\pm\rightarrow J/\psi\,\phi K^\pm decays produced in \bar{p} p  collisions at \sqrt{s}=1.96~\TeV is reported with a statistical significance greater than 5 standard deviations. A fit to the J/\psi\,\phi mass spectrum is performed assuming the presence of a Breit-Wigner resonance. The fit yields a signal of 19^{+6}_{-5} resonance events, and resonance mass and width of

4143.4^{+2.9}_{-3.0}(\mathrm{stat})\pm0.6(\mathrm{syst})~\MeVcc and 15.3^{+10.4}_{-6.1}(\mathrm{stat})\pm2.5(\mathrm{syst})~\MeVcc respectively. The parameters of this resonance-like structure are consistent with values reported from an earlier CDF analysis.
