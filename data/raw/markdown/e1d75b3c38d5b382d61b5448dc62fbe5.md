# pandas.DatetimeIndex.month#

- property DatetimeIndex.month[source]#
- The month as January=1, December=12. See also 
  - `DatetimeIndex.year`
  - The year of the datetime.
  - `DatetimeIndex.day`
  - The day of the datetime.
 Examples >>> datetime_series = pd.Series( ... pd.date_range("2000-01-01", periods=3, freq="ME") ... ) >>> datetime_series 0 2000-01-31 1 2000-02-29 2 2000-03-31 dtype: datetime64[us] >>> datetime_series.dt.month 0 1 1 2 2 3 dtype: int32
