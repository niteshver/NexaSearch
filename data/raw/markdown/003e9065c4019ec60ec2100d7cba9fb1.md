#   ⟨50⟩ UK Local-Authority Property-Market Indicators 
Open, reproducible residential property-market indicators for every local authority in England and Wales, derived from HM Land Registry Price Paid Data.
One record per local-authority district: transaction volume, median and quartile prices, mean price, new-build share, leasehold share and property-type mix, for the 2025 calendar year.

##   ⟨46⟩ Method, and what the numbers are not 
Only **standard price-paid transactions** (PPD category A) are counted, so the figures exclude repossessions, buy-to-let portfolio transfers and other non-market or bulk sales. This is therefore a market **activity** indicator set (what sold, and for how much, in the open market this year) and explicitly _not_ a dwelling-stock census: it says nothing about homes that did not transact. Local authority is taken from the Price Paid `district` field, so no external postcode lookup is needed and every transaction with a district is attributed.
##   ⟨47⟩ Files 
  * `data/la_property_indicators.json` — full indicators incl. property-type mix
  * `data/la_property_indicators.csv` — flat table
  * `scripts/build_indicators.py` — reproducible build from the raw Price Paid CSV
