#   ⟨48⟩ FlyRank Internship — Lane Datasets (Pseudonymized) 
Six task-focused, pseudonymized lanes derived from the FlyRank warehouse. Salted/namespaced hashed keys join across lanes and the warehouse release.
> **Provenance:** these are fixed example cuts prepared in June 2026 (90-day windows as of their build date). They exist to show the _shape_ of lane work — the warehouse release⟨49⟩ (v2 full history) is the source of truth, and capstone-grade features should be engineered from it directly (see starter notebook 03).
