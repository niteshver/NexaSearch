    # Title:Optimal asymptotic bounds for spherical designs

View PDF HTML (experimental)
            Abstract:In this paper we prove the conjecture of Korevaar and Meyers: for each N≥cdtd there exists a spherical t-design in the sphere Sd consisting of N points, where cd is a constant depending only on d.
