# Title:On b-functions of linear free divisors

View PDF HTML (experimental)
            Abstract:  We describe b-functions of linear free divisors and use these results to prove that the logarithmic comparison theorem holds for Koszul free reductive linear free divisors exactly if they are (strongly) Euler homogeneous.
