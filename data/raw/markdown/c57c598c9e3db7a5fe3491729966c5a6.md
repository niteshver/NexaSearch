    # Title:Plucker forms and the theta map

View PDF HTML (experimental)
            Abstract:In this paper we introduce the elementary notion of Plücker form of a pair (E,S), where E is a vector bundle of rank r on a smooth, irreducible, complex projective variety X and S⊂H0(E) is a subspace of dimension rm. We apply this notion to the study of theta map θr on the moduli space SUX(r,0) of semistable vector bundles of rank r and trivial determinant on a curve X of genus g. We prove that θr is generically injective if X is general and g>>r.
