# pandas.api.typing.Resampler.get_group#

- 
Resampler.get_group(*name* )[source]#
- Construct DataFrame from group with provided name. This method retrieves all rows belonging to a specific group identified by the given name. 
  - Parameters:
    - **name** object
    - The name of the group to get as a DataFrame.
  - Returns:
    - Series or DataFrame
    - Get the respective Series or DataFrame corresponding to the group provided.
