    # Title:Laws of large numbers for eigenvectors and eigenvalues associated to random subspaces in a tensor product

View PDF HTML (experimental)
            Abstract:Given two positive integers n and k and a parameter t∈(0,1), we choose at random a vector subspace Vn⊂ℂk⊗ℂn of dimension N∼tnk. We show that the set of k-tuples of singular values of all unit vectors in Vn fills asymptotically (as n tends to infinity) a deterministic convex set Kk,t that we describe using a new norm in $\R^k$.

Our proof relies on free probability, random matrix theory, complex analysis and matrix analysis techniques. The main result result comes together with a law of large numbers for the singular value decomposition of the eigenvectors corresponding to large eigenvalues of a random truncation of a matrix with high eigenvalue degeneracy.
