##   ⟨37⟩ Layout 

```
corpus_dir_jsonl/{train,train_stage1,eval}.jsonl
subsets/<subset>/
  corpus_dir_jsonl/{train,train_stage1,eval}.jsonl
  manifests/audio_manifest.tsv
  audio_parquet/part-xxxxx.parquet

```

`corpus_dir_jsonl/` contains the full un-split corpus for `nan`. `subsets/` keeps the source-set grouping used for selected corpus files, manifests, and audio parquet shards.
##   ⟨38⟩ Rules 
  * Preserve `original_audio_filepath` in every corpus row.
  * Rewrite `audio_filepath` to `subsets/<subset>/audio/<source_set>/<sha shard>/<audio_id>.<stored_ext>`.
  * Convert WAV to FLAC before writing Parquet.
  * Keep existing FLAC/MP3/Opus/Ogg codecs unless explicitly changed later.
  * Use deterministic `audio_id` and SHA shard paths to avoid basename collisions.
