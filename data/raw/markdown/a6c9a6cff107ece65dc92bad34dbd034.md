    # Title:Complete Calabi-Yau metrics from P^2 # 9 \bar P^2

View PDF HTML (experimental)
            Abstract:Let XX denote the complex projective plane, blown up at the nine base points of a pencil of cubics, and let DD be any fiber of the resulting elliptic fibration on XX. Using ansatz metrics inspired by work of Gross-Wilson and a PDE method due to Tian-Yau, we prove that X \setminus DX∖D admits complete Ricci-flat Kähler metrics in most de Rham cohomology classes. If DD is smooth, the metrics converge to split flat cylinders \R^+ \times S^1 \times D$\R^+ \times S^1 \times D$ at an exponential rate. In this case, we also obtain a partial uniqueness result and a local description of the Einstein moduli space, which contains cylindrical metrics whose cross-section does not split off a circle. If DD is singular but of finite monodromy, they converge at least quadratically to flat T^2T2-submersions over flat 2-dimensional cones which need not be quotients of \R^2$\R^2$. If DD is singular of infinite monodromy, their volume growth rates are 4/3 and 2 for the Kodaira types {\rm I}_bIb and {{\rm I}_b}^*Ib∗, their injectivity radii decay like r^{-1/3}r−1/3 and (\log r)^{-1/2}(logr)−1/2, and their curvature tensors decay like r^{-2}r−2 and r^{-2}(\log r)^{-1}r−2(logr)−1. In particular, the {\rm I}_bIb examples show that the curvature estimate from Cheeger-Tian \cite{ct-einstein} cannot be improved in general.
