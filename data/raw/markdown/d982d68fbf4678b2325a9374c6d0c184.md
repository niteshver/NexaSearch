    # Title:A Minimal Lamination with Cantor Set-Like Singularities

View PDF HTML (experimental)
            Abstract:Given a compact closed subset MM of a line segment in \mathbb{R}^3ℝ3, we construct a sequence of minimal surfaces \Sigma_kΣk embedded in a neighborhood CC of the line segment that converge smoothly to a limit lamination of CC away from MM. Moreover, the curvature of this sequence blows up precisely on MM, and the limit lamination has non-removable singularities precisely on the boundary of MM.
