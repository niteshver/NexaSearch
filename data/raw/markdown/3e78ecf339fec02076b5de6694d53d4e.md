    # Title:Annotated Bibliography of Some Papers on Combining Significances or p-values

View PDF HTML (experimental)
            Abstract:  A question that comes up repeatedly is how to combine the results of two experiments if all that is known is that one experiment had a n-sigma effect and another experiment had a m-sigma effect. This question is not well-posed: depending on what additional assumptions are made, the preferred answer is different. The note lists some of the more prominent papers on the topic, with some brief comments and excerpts.
