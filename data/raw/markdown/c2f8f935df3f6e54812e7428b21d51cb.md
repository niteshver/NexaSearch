#   ⟨56⟩ D15-annotated 
CoT-annotated multi-label defect detection & typing — **2,684** records (train=2684) of the corrected `AI4Manufacturing/D15`⟨57⟩ (DefectSpectrum), with teacher-written reasoning (`reasoning`). The query/answer formats were redesigned for foundation-model training (see below); the original D15 `query`/`annot` strings are _not_ reused.
> The repository name is an internal task code. See **Provenance** below.
> **Query diversity (2026-07-11).** The `query` field is drawn from a pool of **40 surface variants** for this task (paraphrases that preserve the task and answer-format exactly; the answer-format directive is held verbatim), each selected by an independent per-record hash. This replaces the earlier 4-template design to prevent instruction-format overfitting; answers, images, ids, and all provenance are unchanged. A machine gate in `verify_*.py` checks that no template correlates with the gold (binomial z < 4.5).
> **Excision (2026-07-17):** one contradiction record removed (image sha256 4be393b8..., `DS-VISION/Capacitor/512.jpg` — source labels it anomalous with an empty mask; derived golds would have taught "good"). The base D15 repo retains it as a disclosed source contradiction. Record count 2,685 → **2,684**. Counts auto-generated by `annotate/D15/verify_d15_family.py --card-numbers` (2026-07-17).
##   ⟨47⟩ What changed vs. D15 (format mutation) 
  * **`query`**— 4 natural-language surface variants (loss is on completions, so input diversity is safe); natural product wording (e.g. "textured industrial surface", never internal codes like "DS-DAGM"); the category's closed defect-class list is stated in the query; asks: good or defective + name every type present + where each appears.
  * **`annot`**— canonical JSON`{"label":"good"|"defective","defects":[{"type","region"}...]}` , defect types in **alphabetical order** (deterministic target = no token-level label noise), `region` a 3×3-grid word ("upper left" … "center") derived from the mask centroid.
  * **`reasoning`**— teacher CoT ending in a canonical line`FINAL ANSWER: good` / `FINAL ANSWER: defective — <type> (<region>), ...` (alphabetical, exact match to the gold — enforced at assembly).


##   ⟨48⟩ How the reasoning was produced (and gated) 
Teacher **`claude-sonnet-5`**(Anthropic Batch, adaptive thinking),**gold-conditioned** : the teacher was shown the verified answer plus non-leaking grounding — per-type region/area derived from the semantic mask, per-type zoom crops, a histogram-equalized full view for DS-DAGM (raw DAGM defects are near-invisible), and DefectSpectrum's human captions where they exist (DS-MVTec) — and wrote an inspection-style justification addressing each defect type separately. Grounding is teacher-side only and never appears in `query`.
Gate = **improve-never-drop** : deterministic checks (leak regex, FINAL-line exact match, per-type justification) + an independent grounded judge (`claude-sonnet-5`, same image + grounding stack, faithfulness 1–5). Flagged records were **regenerated with the critique fed back** , re-judged, and the better version kept; any residual FINAL-line deviation is spliced to the exact gold line. Nothing is dropped; per-record provenance lives in `metadata.cot` (`method` ∈ rationalized / rationalized_regenerated / rationalized+final_spliced, plus the judge verdict).
**Run stats (full corpus, 2026-07-08).** 2,685/2,685 annotated — nothing dropped:  
| method  | n  | meaning  |  
| --- | --- | --- |  
| `rationalized`  | 2,236  | first-pass CoT passed all deterministic checks + judge faith ≥3  |  
| `rationalized_regenerated`  | 327  | regenerated with the judge's critique; passed on re-judge  |  
| `rationalized+final_spliced`  | 122  | best available prose kept; exact gold `FINAL ANSWER` line enforced programmatically  |  
Judge faithfulness on the kept CoTs: mean 3.92/5 — 1:1 (0.0%), 2:77 (2.9%), 3:546 (20.3%), 4:1569 (58.5%), 5:491 (18.3%) (each verdict describes the actual kept prose: for regenerated-then-spliced records the re-judge verdict is stored, not the original screen's). Counts auto-generated by `annotate/D15/verify_d15_family.py --card-numbers` (2026-07-17). The 78 records the judge still scores ≤2 and 49 records whose prose doesn't name every gold type verbatim are **kept with their verdicts in`metadata.cot`** — filter on `metadata.cot.judge.faith` if you want a stricter subset. Deterministic guarantees hold for **all** records regardless: FINAL line == gold, `annot` re-derived from the mask at assembly, zero grounding leaks.
> **Default filter:** exclude `metadata.cot.judge.faith <= 2` (78 records) from `reasoning`-imitation training; their prose contains judge-asserted perceptual errors chained to correct FINAL answers.
> **Known property:** ~60% of CoTs close with completeness claims ("no other defects...") over GT that is not certified exhaustive — a hedged-closure policy applies to newer datasets; treat unhedged closures as stylistic, not factual. Judge calls ran on `claude-sonnet-5` via the ephone gateway (sync); teacher + regen ran on the Anthropic Batch API.

##   ⟨50⟩ Roles 
**Roles:** `reasoning` is the SFT imitation target — its `FINAL ANSWER` segment is the model-facing answer format; `annot` is the machine-parseable gold used for verification and reward parsing, **not** an output-format target.
##   ⟨51⟩ Grounded traces 
The `reasoning_grounded` column was removed 2026-07-18 per the corpus grounded-trace doctrine: for classification-shape tasks the trace can only restate the verdict (with unrequested coordinates) — geometry lives in `metadata`; traces are pure functions of the GT and the committed generator (forge_model `annotate/D15/`) and can be re-rendered on demand. Default SFT view: `query` -> `reasoning`. RLVR uses `query` + `annot`.
##   ⟨52⟩ Provenance 
Underlying dataset: **DefectSpectrum** (EnVision-Research, ECCV 2024, arXiv:2310.17316) via `AI4Manufacturing/D15`⟨57⟩ **after its 2026-07-08 correction** (26 upstream-misfiled `pill/good` metal-nut photos removed; see D15's `SOURCE_DATA_ISSUES.md`). Annotation pipeline: `annotate/D15/annotate_d15.py` + `annotate/D15/d15_pipeline.py` in `AI4Manufacturing/forge_model`⟨58⟩. Upstream license: MIT (respect the underlying datasets' terms; this card is `license: other`).
##   ⟨53⟩ Training-mixture notes (2026-07-17 certification) 
  * One photo appears in up to 5 family artifacts (family mean ≈6.3 items/photo). Carve any train/eval split **PHOTO-WISE** on `metadata.image_sha256` across ALL D15-family repos simultaneously.
  * Global label prior: 85.6% of photos are defective — a blind guesser scores ≈90% on good-vs-defective labels. Counterweight with good-heavy sources in your mixture; this is a structural property of DefectSpectrum, not a leak (text-only blind probe on shipped queries: MCQ at exact chance 25%; region at prior level).
  * 641 VISION photos in this family are byte-shared with `AI4Manufacturing/D23`⟨59⟩ under a **MATERIALLY DIFFERENT label policy** (DS masks confirm only 88.5% of VISION boxes; worst subset coverage 0.03). Take each shared image from exactly ONE side — machine-readable keys: `overlap_with_D23.json` on the `D15`⟨57⟩ repo. Full sibling-overlap table: D15 base card §8.


##   ⟨54⟩ Overlap / de-duplication (§8) 
Same base images as D15 → inherits all of D15's sha-verified overlaps: DS-MVTec (1,616) ⊂ `D20`⟨60⟩ **test** (94% of it; same images also in `D05`⟨61⟩); DS-DAGM (270) ⊂ `181`⟨62⟩ (120 in 181's test); DS-VISION (717) ⊂ `D23`⟨59⟩ (incl. 326 val). **Do not evaluate on those repos' held-out splits if you train on this set.** Reconstruct exact overlap sets via `metadata.image_sha256`.
##   ⟨55⟩ Training notes 
  * Loss on completions; default SFT view: `query` -> `reasoning`. `annot` is the machine-parseable gold (verification / RLVR reward parsing), not an output-format target.
  * Companion set: `D15-mcq`⟨63⟩ — deterministic mask-MCQ (Set-of-Mark) built from the same masks, exact-match gradable (RLVR-ready).
