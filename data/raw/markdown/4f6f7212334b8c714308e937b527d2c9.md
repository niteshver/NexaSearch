    # Title:An isomorphism between the completion of an Algebra and its Caratheodory Extension

View PDF HTML (experimental)
            Abstract:  Let Ω denote an algebra of sets and μ a σ-finite measure. We then prove that the completion of Ω under the pseudometric d(A,B) = μ∗(A△B) is σ-algebra isomorphic and isometric to the Caratheodory Extension of Ω under the equivalence relation ∼.
