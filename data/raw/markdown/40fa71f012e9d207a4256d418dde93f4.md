#  Title:A Spectral Approach to Analyzing Belief Propagation for 3-Coloring
Authors:Amin Coja-Oghlan, Elchanan Mossel, Dan Vilenchik
View a PDF of the paper titled A Spectral Approach to Analyzing Belief Propagation for 3-Coloring, by Amin Coja-Oghlan and Elchanan Mossel and Dan Vilenchik
View PDF
> Abstract: Contributing to the rigorous understanding of BP, in this paper we relate the convergence of BP to spectral properties of the graph. This encompasses a result for random graphs with a ``planted'' solution; thus, we obtain the first rigorous result on BP for graph coloring in the case of a complex graphical structure (as opposed to trees). In particular, the analysis shows how Belief Propagation breaks the symmetry between the possible permutations of the color classes.
