# pandas.PeriodIndex.is_leap_year#

- property PeriodIndex.is_leap_year[source]#
- Logical indicating if the date belongs to a leap year. See also 
  - `PeriodIndex.qyear`
  - Fiscal year the Period lies in according to its starting-quarter.
  - `PeriodIndex.year`
  - The year of the period.
 Examples >>> idx = pd.PeriodIndex(["2023", "2024", "2025"], freq="Y") >>> idx.is_leap_year array([False, True, False])
