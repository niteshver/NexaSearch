    # Title:The algebra of cell-zeta values

View PDF HTML (experimental)
            Abstract:  In this paper, we introduce cell-forms on \mathcal{M}_{0,n}0,n, which are top-dimensional differential forms diverging along the boundary of exactly one cell (connected component) of the real moduli space \mathcal{M}_{0,n}(\mathbb{R})0,n(ℝ). We show that the cell-forms generate the top-dimensional cohomology group of \mathcal{M}_{0,n}0,n, so that there is a natural duality between cells and cell-forms. In the heart of the paper, we determine an explicit basis for the subspace of differential forms which converge along a given cell XX. The elements of this basis are called insertion forms, their integrals over XX are real numbers, called cell-zeta values, which generate a \mathbb{Q}ℚ-algebra called the cell-zeta algebra. By a result of F. Brown, the cell-zeta algebra is equal to the algebra of multizeta values. The cell-zeta values satisfy a family of simple quadratic relations coming from the geometry of moduli spaces, which leads to a natural definition of a formal version of the cell-zeta algebra, conjecturally isomorphic to the formal multizeta algebra defined by the much-studied double shuffle relations.
