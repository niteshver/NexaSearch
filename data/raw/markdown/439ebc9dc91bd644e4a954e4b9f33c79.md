    # Title:Experimental study of the decay K_L^0\toπ^0ν\barν

View PDF HTML (experimental)
            Abstract:  The first dedicated search for the rare neutral-kaon decay K_L^0\to\pi^0\nu\bar{\nu} has been carried out in the E391a experiment at the KEK 12-GeV proton synchrotron. The final upper limit of 2.6 \times 10^{-8} at the 90% confidence level was set on the branching ratio for the decay.
