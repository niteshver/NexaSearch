    # Title:Combining Approximation Algorithms for the Prize-Collecting TSP

View PDF HTML (experimental)
            Abstract:  We present a 1.91457-approximation algorithm for the prize-collecting travelling salesman problem. This is obtained by combining a randomized variant of a rounding algorithm of Bienstock et al. and a primal-dual algorithm of Goemans and Williamson.
