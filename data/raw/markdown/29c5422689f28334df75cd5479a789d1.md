    # Title:Curves over higher local fields

View PDF HTML (experimental)
            Abstract:  In this work, we prove the vanishing of the two cohomological group of the higher local field. This generalize the well-known propriety of finite field and one dimensional local field. We apply this result to study the arithmetic of curve defined over higher local field.
