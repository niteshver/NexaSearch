    # Title:A meteorite crater on Mt. Ararat?

View PDF
            Abstract:We briefly report on a crater on the western slope of this http URL . It is located in an area closed to foreigners at an altitude around 2100m with geographic coordinates 39°47' 30"N, 44°14' 40"E. The diameter of the crater is around 60-70m, the depth is up to 15m. The origin of the crater, either of meteorite impact or volcanic, including the evaluation of its age, will need detailed studies.
