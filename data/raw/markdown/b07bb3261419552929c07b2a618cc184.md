Tutorials are an opportunity for ACL-IJCNLP attendees, both newcomers and old hands, to be introduced
to various topics by people doing cutting-edge research in those topics. As in previous years, tutorials
were selected by a uniﬁed review process across four conferences (ACL-IJCNLP, EACL, NAACL, and
EMNLP). This year, we received 35 submissions, of which six were selected for presentation at ACL-
IJCNLP 2021. We’re very pleased to have tutorials from experts from all around the world on a diverse
range of applications and techniques in NLP, and hope they will be of great beneﬁt to our community.
We would like to thank Xiangyu Duan for preparing this proceedings volume, and Jing-Shin Chang,
Yvette Graham, and Yuki Arase for their hard work coordinating the publications process.
