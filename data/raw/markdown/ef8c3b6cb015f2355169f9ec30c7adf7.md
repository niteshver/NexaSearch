# pandas.DatetimeTZDtype#

- 
*class* pandas.DatetimeTZDtype(*unit='ns'* ,*tz=None* )[source]#
- An ExtensionDtype for timezone-aware datetime data. **This is not an actual numpy dtype** , but a duck type.
  - Parameters:
    - **unit** str, default “ns”
    - The precision of the datetime data. Currently limited to `"ns"` .
    - **tz** str, int, or datetime.tzinfo
    - The timezone.
  - Raises:
    - ZoneInfoNotFoundError
    - When the requested timezone cannot be found.
 Examples >>> from zoneinfo import ZoneInfo >>> pd.DatetimeTZDtype(tz=ZoneInfo('UTC')) datetime64[ns, UTC] >>> pd.DatetimeTZDtype(tz=ZoneInfo('Europe/Paris')) datetime64[ns, Europe/Paris] Attributes `unit`The precision of the datetime data. `tz`The timezone. Methods **None**
