    # Title:Principalization of ideals in abelian extensions of number fields

View PDF HTML (experimental)
            Abstract:  We give the complete proof of a conjecture of Georges Gras which claims that, for any extension K/k of number fields in which at least one infinite place is totally split, every ideal I of K principalizes in the compositum Kkab of K with the maximal abelian extension kab of k
