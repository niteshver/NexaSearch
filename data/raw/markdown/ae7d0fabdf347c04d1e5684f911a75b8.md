    # Title:All electrical measurement of the density of states in (Ga,Mn)As

View PDF HTML (experimental)
            Abstract:  We report on electrical measurements of the effective density of states in the ferromagnetic semiconductor material (Ga,Mn)As. By analyzing the conductivity correction due to enhanced electron-electron interaction the electrical diffusion constant was extracted for (Ga,Mn)As samples of different dimensionality. Using the Einstein relation allows to deduce the effective density of states of (Ga,Mn)As at the Fermi energy.
