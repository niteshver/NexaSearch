# pandas.DatetimeTZDtype.unit#

- property DatetimeTZDtype.unit[source]#
- The precision of the datetime data. Returns the time resolution as one of `'s'` ,`'ms'` ,`'us'` ,
or`'ns'` .See also 
  - `DatetimeTZDtype.tz`
  - Retrieves the timezone.
 Examples >>> from zoneinfo import ZoneInfo >>> dtype = pd.DatetimeTZDtype(tz=ZoneInfo("America/Los_Angeles")) >>> dtype.unit 'ns'
