    # Title:On the product of vector spaces in a commutative field extension

View PDF HTML (experimental)
            Abstract:  Let K \subset LK⊂L be a commutative field extension. Given KK-subspaces A,BA,B of LL, we consider the subspace <AB><AB> spanned by the product set AB=\{ab \mid a \in A, b \in B\}AB={ab∣a∈A,b∈B}. If \dim_K A = rdimKA=r and \dim_K B = sdimKB=s, how small can the dimension of <AB><AB> be? In this paper we give a complete answer to this question in characteristic 0, and more generally for separable extensions. The optimal lower bound on \dim_K < AB>dimK<AB> turns out, in this case, to be provided by the numerical function  \kappa_{K,L}(r,s) = \min_{h} (\lceil r/h\rceil + \lceil s/h\rceil -1)h, where h runs over the set of K-dimensions of all finite-dimensional intermediate fields K \subset H \subset L. This bound is closely related to one appearing in additive number theory.
