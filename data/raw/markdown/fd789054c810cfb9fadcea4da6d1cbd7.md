    # Title:Polynomiality of some hook-length statistics

View PDF HTML (experimental)
            Abstract:We prove a conjecture of Okada giving an exact formula for a certain statistic for hook-lengths of partitions:
\frac{1}{n!} \sum_{\lambda \vdash n} f_{\lambda}^2 \sum_{u \in \lambda} \prod_{i=1}^{r}(h_u^2 - i^2) = \frac{1}{2(r+1)^2} \binom{2r}{r}\binom{2r+2}{r+1} \prod_{j=0}^{r} (n-j),
where fλ is the number of standard Young tableaux of shape λ and hu is the hook length of the square u of the Young diagram of λ. We also obtain other similar formulas.
