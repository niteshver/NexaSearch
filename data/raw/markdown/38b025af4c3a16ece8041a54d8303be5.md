    # Title:A Simple Correctness Proof for Magic Transformation

View PDF HTML (experimental)
            Abstract:The paper presents a simple and concise proof of correctness of the magic transformation. We believe it may provide a useful example of formal reasoning about logic programs.

The correctness property concerns the declarative semantics. The proof, however, refers to the operational semantics (LD-resolution) of the source programs. Its conciseness is due to applying a suitable proof method.
