    # Title:Group-invariant solutions of a nonlinear acoustics model

View PDF HTML (experimental)
            Abstract:Based on a recent classification of subalgebras of the symmetry algebra of the Zabolotskaya-Khokhlov equation, all similarity reductions of this equation into ordinary differential equations are obtained. Large classes of group-invariant solutions of the equation are also determined, and some properties of the reduced equations and exact solutions are discussed.
