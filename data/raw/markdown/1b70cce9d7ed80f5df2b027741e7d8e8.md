    # Title:Osserman and conformally Osserman manifolds with warped and twisted product structure

View PDF HTML (experimental)
            Abstract:  We characterize Osserman and conformally Osserman Riemannian manifolds with the local structure of a warped product. By means of this approach we analyze the twisted product structure and obtain, as a consequence, that the only Osserman manifolds which can be written as a twisted product are those of constant curvature.
