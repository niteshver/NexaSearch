    # Title:Comments on ``Spin Connection Resonance in Gravitational General Relativity''

View PDF HTML (experimental)
            Abstract:  We comment on a recent article of this http URL, Acta Physica Polonica B38 (2007) 2211. We point out that the equations underlying Evans' theory are highly problematic. Moreover, we demonstrate that the so-called ``spin connection resonance'', predicted by Evans, cannot be derived from the equation he used. We provide an exact solution of Evans' corresponding equation and show that is has definitely no resonance solutions.
