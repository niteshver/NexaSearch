    # Title:The Acceleration of the Expansion of the Universe: A Brief Early History of the Supernova Cosmology Project (SCP)

View PDF HTML (experimental)
            Abstract:  It is now about 10 years since the evidence, based on Type Ia supernovae, for the acceleration of the expansion of the Universe was discovered. I will discuss some aspects of the work and events in the Supernova Cosmology Project (SCP), during the period 1988 to 1998, which led to this discovery.
