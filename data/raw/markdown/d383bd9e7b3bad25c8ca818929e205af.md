#   ⟨58⟩ Video SAR - Optical Image Registration Dataset 
##   ⟨49⟩ 1. Dataset Overview 
This dataset provides terahertz (THz) video SAR and optical image pairs with certain registration accuracy for cross-modal remote sensing research. It covers multiple typical scenes and SAR imaging modes, and can serve as benchmark data for heterogeneous image registration and matching algorithm validation.
**Important Limitation Note** : The terahertz video Synthetic Aperture Radar observation data contained in this benchmark dataset are all acquired from airborne field imaging experiments. At present, the research on THz SAR-to-optical image registration is still in an immature stage. Combined with the restrictions brought by the unique physical imaging mechanism of the terahertz band, the original imaging quality and cross-modal registration accuracy of the dataset have objective inherent defects. Researchers can carry out targeted data modification, sample expansion and customized preprocessing based on this dataset to adapt to their differentiated research objectives and experimental schemes. 
##   ⟨50⟩ 2. Dataset Composition 
###   ⟨51⟩ 2.1 Imaging modes 
Spotlight mode and Stripmap mode
###   ⟨52⟩ 2.2 Typical scenes 
  1. Airport (Spotlight)
  2. Sundial (Spotlight)
  3. Village (Stripmap)


###   ⟨53⟩ 2.3 Data characteristics 
Data form: Continuous video frame sequences with registration correspondence. Each scene contains hundreds of sequential frames, with thousands of valid registration pairs in total, supporting temporal stability analysis of registration algorithms.
##   ⟨54⟩ 3. Application Scenarios 
This dataset is applicable to research including:
  * Cross-modal image registration and matching
  * Multi-source remote sensing data fusion
  * Video SAR target tracking
  * Heterogeneous image feature extraction and alignment algorithm validation
