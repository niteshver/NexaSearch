    # Title:Computing faithful representations for nilpotent Lie algebras

View PDF HTML (experimental)
            Abstract:  We describe three methods to determine a faithful representation of small dimension for a finite-dimensional nilpotent Lie algebra over an arbitrary field. We apply our methods in finding bounds for the smallest dimension \mu(\Lg) of a faithful \Lg-module for some nilpotent Lie algebras \Lg. In particular, we describe an infinite family of filiform nilpotent Lie algebras \Lf_n of dimension n over \Q and conjecture that \mu(\Lf_n) > n+1. Experiments with our algorithms suggest that \mu(\Lf_n) is polynomial in n.
