    # Title:The Corona Theorem for the Drury-Arveson Hardy space and other holomorphic Besov-Sobolev spaces on the unit ball in \mathbb{C}^{n}

View PDF HTML (experimental)
            Abstract:  We prove that the multiplier algebra of the Drury-Arveson Hardy space H_{n}^{2} on the unit ball in \mathbb{C}^{n} has no corona in its maximal ideal space, thus generalizing the famous Corona Theorem of L. Carleson to higher dimensions. This result is obtained as a corollary of the Toeplitz corona theorem and a new Banach space result: the Besov-Sobolev space B_{p}^{\sigma} has the "baby corona property" for all \sigma \geq 0 and 1<p<\infty . In addition we obtain infinite generator and semi-infinite matrix versions of these theorems.
