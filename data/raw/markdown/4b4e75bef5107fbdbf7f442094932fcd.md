    # Title:Local conditions for global representations of quadratic forms

View PDF HTML (experimental)
            Abstract:  We show that the theorem of Ellenberg and Venkatesh on representation of integral quadratic forms by integral positive definite quadratic forms is valid under weaker conditions on the represented form.
