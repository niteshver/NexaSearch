    # Title:Note on a theorem of Bousfield and Friedlander

View PDF HTML (experimental)
            Abstract:  We examine the proof of a classical localization theorem of Bousfield and Friedlander and we remove the assumption that the underlying model category be right proper. The key to the argument is a lemma about factoring in morphisms in the arrow category of a model category.
