    # Title:The Asymmetric Superfluid Local Density Approximation (ASLDA)

View PDF HTML (experimental)
            Abstract:  Here we describe the form of the Asymmetric Superfluid Local Density Approximation (ASLDA), a Density Functional Theory (DFT) used to model the two-component unitary Fermi gas. We give the rational behind the functional, and describe explicitly how we determine the form of the DFT from the to the available numerical and experimental data.
