    # Title:Some Remarks on the Braided Thompson Group BV

View PDF HTML (experimental)
            Abstract:  Matthew Brin and Patrick Dehornoy independently discovered a braided version BV of Thompson's group V. In this paper, we discuss some properties of BV that might make the group interesting for group based cryptography. In particular, we show that BV does not admit a non-trivial linear representation.
