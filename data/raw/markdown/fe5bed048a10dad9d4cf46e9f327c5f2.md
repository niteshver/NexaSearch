  * **Improvements over stock Qwen2.5-1.5B** (measured): output-marker compliance 71%→100%, cross-lingual output language obeyed, map-reduce merge without hallucinated figures.
  * **License** : Apache-2.0 (inherits Qwen2.5). Fine-tune data generated synthetically.
